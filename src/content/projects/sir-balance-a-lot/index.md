## Elevator Pitch
Learning project to learn and experiment with reinforcement learning, simulation and sim-to-real transfer.
System is a slightly modified cart-pole example, where the rod to balance is attached to an actuated disk.

This project is essentially open-ended, with the goal to learn, implement and experiment 
with whatever seems most interesting at the time.

## Goal
Overarching goal is to develop full pipeline of simulation, RL and sim-to-real transfer.

## Accomplishments
*Ongoing project, most goals not yet reached or even defined.*
- [x] Self-developed simulation of physical system, including visualization
- [x] First demo of successful PPO RL control policy (implemented by [@sgeisser1](https://sgeisser1.github.io/))
- [ ] Hardware design & assembly of physical system
- [ ] Vision system for state estimation

## Skills
- Equations of motion
- Simulation
- Reinforcement Learning
